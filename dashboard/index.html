Every protocol DefiLlama tracks, ranked by what its launch would be worth — if its record stayed as it is.

How the value is worked out

implied value = TVL × 0.30 × growth × maturity × breadth

  • Growth — the protocol's 7-day TVL change, clamped to −50%…+150%, weighted 0.8.
  • Maturity — 0.70 for a brand-new listing, rising to 1.20 after 4 years with no token.
  • Breadth — 1.00 on one chain, +0.05 per extra chain, up to 1.20.
  • Nothing else. No name is special-cased; the same four numbers score every row.

An implied value is this board's own claim from the sealed snapshot. It is not a prediction that a token will launch, or when — only what one would be worth if the protocol's record stayed as it is.
